Best starting strategy: Pick one relationship community (Alumni/SOM clubs), one capability center (Tsai CITY, Yale Ventures, or a sector center), and one mission lane (Fox, Jackson, Tobin, or public service). Put an owner and a next action on each before the first residency. Confirm access with your EMBA program team: many Yale opportunities are open to the wider university, while selective fellowships, courses, and competitions may have specific eligibility rules.

Two-year operating plan

Weeks 1–6 · orient

Map your people

Turn access into conversations.

  • Activate SOM alumni profile
  • Book CDO + library orientations
  • Join one student club
Months 2–9 · explore

Test your thesis

Use Yale’s intellectual edges.

  • Attend center events
  • Meet faculty and practitioners
  • Try a case or pitch competition
Months 10–18 · build

Make a contribution

Become useful to a community.

  • Lead a club initiative
  • Publish, present, or advise
  • Apply for selective programs
Months 19–24 · compound

Leave a bridge

Convert relationships into legacy.

  • Mentor a new cohort
  • Document your Yale network
  • Stay active as an alum
